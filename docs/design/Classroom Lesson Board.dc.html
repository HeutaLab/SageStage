Modelling board — I do · we do · you doMockups · built on your Story Map screens

Everything here reuses the chrome you already have — dark board frame, Story Map card, the Model · Together · Over-to-you bar, word bank, boxing-up paper, emotion graph. What is new is the machinery that makes the hand-off visible, and the places where a teacher's ad-hoc brilliance currently falls on the floor.

One dial runs the lesson

How much help is on sits in the stage bar. It moves itself when you change stage, and you can drag it back. It is the single control that covers the word bank, shrinks the criteria and hides the model — so scaffolds never fade by accident.

Catch the brilliance

The word panel is live in every stage and now records who said it. A word offered at 10:42 by Amira is in the bank, on the board with her name on it, and in tomorrow's plan by the time she sits down.

Runs for a group, not just a class

The Whole class pill switches the board to an EAL or SEN group. A TA gets the same board with more help on, stems showing, and the teacher's notes swapped for TA prompts.

Assumed: two surfaces only (classroom board + teacher tablet) · children write in books, on whiteboards or on paper — never on a device · one lesson, one section of the story mountain · Year 4 shown, sizes hold from Y1 to Y6 · nothing auto-advances, the teacher moves every stage by hand.
