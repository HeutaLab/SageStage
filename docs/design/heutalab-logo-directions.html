WHERE THIS LANDEDC · The curious spark

Glenn flagged the spark twice during this review, and the email header shipped in colour on 2 Aug. Still open — scope. Either the spark becomes the mark everywhere (favicon, @heutalab avatars, the heutalab.com header — the four bars stay in the family as tile art, where they already live), or the spark stays on comms while a glyph — A’s bars or B’s Eta — does favicon-and-avatar duty. Everywhere is the tidier brand. Say the word and the kit follows: favicon set, avatars for Instagram / X / YouTube / TikTok, GitHub org avatar, channel banners, OG image, the header swap, and footer marks for sagestage.app and sagestage.co.uk so the maker is visible in the corner of everything.

Comps built from the estate's own materials: colours from heutalab.com's tokens, wordmark set in the vendored Poppins (700 for the name, 600 for the tagline) converted to paths — no fonts to install, nothing fetched. Every mark here is already a working SVG.
