why this shape

  • The number pad is silent, exact, and works in a loud room. No recognition to get wrong in front of thirty children.
  • Four flow buttons are thumb-findable without looking down.
  • Hold-to-talk is the only truly eyes-free control, so it earns the biggest target — and the phone can only ever be the button; the microphone is the laptop's (§6.1).
  • “Sending to” names the resolved widget, so a tapped or spoken number never lands somewhere surprising (§3.3).
  • The pad and the voice produce the same intent. That is the whole point of the command layer (§3.1).
Open question §12.4: whether this needs the widget list at all, or only flow plus a number pad. More surface is more to look at, and looking down is the thing being avoided.
